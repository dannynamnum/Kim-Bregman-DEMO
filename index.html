Meet Kim

Forty years of fiduciary duty to buyers.

Kim Bregman is the only Certified Exclusive Buyer Agent serving Palm Beach and Broward Counties — and has been for over four decades. She has never represented a seller. Not once.

That distinction matters. In a Florida market where 99.9% of agents work as transactional agents, Kim owes a single, undivided fiduciary duty — to the buyer. She acts as advocate, researcher, advisor, and negotiator, with no conflicts and no divided loyalties.

A second-generation native Floridian with an MBA from the University of Florida, Kim founded Optima Properties in 1985 and has built a four-decade business almost entirely on client referrals.

The Approach

Why work with an Exclusive Buyer Agent.

In Florida, 99.9% of real estate agents are transactional — meaning they owe no fiduciary duty to either side. Exclusive Buyer Agency is different by design.

Single Agency

Never a seller's side, ever.

Every recommendation, every negotiation, every piece of advice is owed entirely to you. No dual agency, no shifting loyalties, no transactional handoffs.

New Construction

A counterweight to the builder's rep.

The on-site sales rep works for the builder. Kim works for you — reviewing incentives, lot premiums, and contracts with four decades of perspective on what's negotiable.

Relocation

Florida, properly explained.

Insurance, building codes, hurricane considerations, HOA quirks — the things long-time Floridians forget to mention. Kim makes sure you know what you're buying into.

Investment & Second Homes

Numbers, not narratives.

With an MBA and four decades of South Florida market data, Kim evaluates rental yield, tax exposure, and resale potential — and tells you when a property doesn't pencil out.
